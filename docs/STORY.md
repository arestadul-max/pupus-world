# Story · কাহিনি

## Premise

**Pupu** is a boy with a big imagination who lives with his Ma, his Baba and his ginger cat **Mishti** in a village house in Bangladesh. Every night he dreams: the people he dislikes come back as enemies, the people he loves come back as friends and helpers.

His beloved grandfather, **Dadu**, has passed away, and Pupu has locked the memories away so they don't hurt. Tonight his dreams start giving them back, one by one. In every chapter Pupu and Mishti find one of **Dadu's ten things**; each one opens a memory of Dadu, and each one goes into Dadu's old painted tin trunk.

The game never explains ahead of time where the road leads. Mishti is Pupu's friend and fellow traveller: she discovers everything *with* him, wonders and encourages, and never knows more than he does.

## Prologue · Midnight TV
11:40 at night. The cartoon movie starts at twelve, and the TV is downstairs. Pupu tries to slip out of his room and Ma catches him straight away, so he has to try again, this time crouching all the way: past Ma's door, past Mishti (who will meow for food unless she gets her treats), behind the window curtain when Ma comes out for water, and back into bed when she checks his room. When he finally switches on the TV at full volume, Ma is not pleased. Back in bed, the bed starts to rock like a boat…

## Chapter 1 · The Ghat at the Edge of Sleep
Pupu wakes in a country boat at a river ghat at sunset. Mishti can suddenly talk. The ghat feels strangely familiar and strangely grey. On the first step an old man's voice says "slowly, you'll slip…", and nobody is there. Small things appear: footprints, two pairs of sandals (one big, one small), a bench someone used to share with him.

At the top, a glowing flat stone hangs from the banyan. Swinging across the roots to catch it opens **memory 1: skipping stones with Dadu at sunset**, a three-round contest. The colour comes back to the ghat. Then lotus leaves, a flying paper boat, a crab, crumbling stones and a naughty crow that steals Pupu's cap lead to Montu-da's tea stall, where Dadu used to sit with Pupu every day, and where Dadu's trunk waits with ten empty places. Walking on, the ground opens into a hole full of chalk numbers…

## Chapter 2 · Escape from Anko Sir's Class
Pupu and Mishti wake up tiny on the floor of a giant maths classroom, ruled by **Anko Sir**, whose glasses shine like searchlights. They crawl under the benches unseen, escape onto the verandah with a classmate, **Jhumpa**, hide from the windows, race down the stairs and across the moonlit schoolyard with Anko Sir striding after them.

At the chained gate he catches them, and finds Pupu's crumpled homework: the last sum crossed out five times and tried again. The giant shrinks to an ordinary teacher. "For trying… here, this is your gift." It is **Dadu's reading glasses**, and **memory 2** opens: Dadu's room at night, little Pupu stuck on a sum, and Dadu saying "আবার চেষ্টা করো" ("Try again"). The player solves the puzzles with Dadu, a sum step by step, a number pattern and a riddle, different every time.

**The bridge to Chapter 3:** back at the gate, a magic sci-fi door crackles out of nowhere, spitting blue sparks. Anko Sir decides he saw nothing and needs some sleep; Jhumpa waits with him; the player walks Pupu into the door, and Mishti follows.

## Chapter 3 · Kalo-Bijli's Lab
Through the door, Pupu and Mishti step into a huge laboratory, floor upon floor, and Pupu knows it at once: it belongs to **Professor Kalo-Bijli**, the villain of his favourite cartoon *Thunder Boy*, who tries to steal Thunder Boy's lightning in every episode. Pupu has always wanted to teach him a lesson. The professor appears on his live screen: "Security system: ON!" A steel shutter slams over the door and a giant eye starts sweeping the room with a red beam.

**Ground floor:** hiding behind crates from the eye, jumping a spark-bug, crossing a bench while the burner is low, Pupu climbs to a storage loft, pushes a trolley over the edge, climbs it and grabs a glowing **battery**, which also powers an old lift. **Machine room:** past a crusher and an electric gate, across a pit on a moving platform, through a crawl tunnel and up a machine to a switch that opens a cabinet: the glowing **knob**. **Upper hall:** up front- and side-facing ladders, an elevator and two platforms hanging on chains to the top of a tall glass tank, where a compartment holds the glowing **antenna**. **Control room:** three glowing slots on Kalo-Bijli's console; Pupu puts the right thing in each, and the professor's **Lightning Collector** powers down ("No, no, NO!"). A wall slides open on a secret room: an old radio with an empty battery box, no knob and no antenna. The three things fit: it is **Dadu's pocket radio**. "That radio's glow powered my whole lab! Give it back!" "It's not yours. It's my Dadu's!" The professor is zapped by his own lightning: "That's your lesson, Professor!" "I'll get you for this, Pupu…"

**Memory 3** opens: an evening at their village house, on the raised mud verandah (dawa) with Dadu, the radio "broken", and little Pupu putting it back together with his own hands while Dadu shows him how: the battery pushed in, the knob screwed on, the antenna pulled out long, then the dial turned slowly through the hiss until a song comes through. "Fix things with care and patience, and even broken things sing again." Back in the lab, the radio still plays: "Mm… it still sings."

## Epilogue · Dadu's room (planned)
Dawn, in the real world. Pupu opens the door of Dadu's room, shut since Dadu passed. All ten things are there. Touching each one brings back a line of Dadu's voice, and the room slowly fills with colour. Ma quietly sits on Dadu's bed; Baba leans in the doorway and tells Pupu that Dadu kept every drawing he ever made. The room is the warm, lamp-lit room of memory 2.


**Dadu's tin trunk:** first seen on Dadu's bench at Montu-da's tea stall (Chapter 1). At the end of every chapter it shimmers into view and Pupu lays the chapter's memory thing inside: the stone (1/10), the glasses (2/10), the radio (3/10)…
