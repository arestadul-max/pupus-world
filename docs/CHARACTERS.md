# Characters · চরিত্র

All characters are drawn in code in the Rongpat style: bold ink outlines, flat colour, a long almond "fish" eye in profile, lively brows and mouths. **Every character shows an expression when they talk** (angry, sad, scared, grinning, suspicious, surprised, happy), and **no character ever slides**: every move uses a real walk or run cycle matched to its speed.

## Pupu · পুপু
The hero, 13–14. Slim, backwards red cap (never in bed), a band-aid on his cheek, red T-shirt, indigo knee shorts; barefoot at home, white canvas sneakers in the dream (barefoot as a little boy in memories). Imaginative, a little mischievous, brave when it counts; he misses Dadu more than he says. His favourite cartoon is *Thunder Boy* (the big poster on his wall).
**Moves:** walk, run (knees high, arms pumping), crouch-walk, jump with anticipation and landing, sit (side and front), squat to pick up, throw, swing on roots, hide, sleep curled up.

## Mishti · মিষ্টি
Pupu's ginger tabby cat: orange coat with dark stripes, cream belly, pink nose, a slim red collar with a little gold bell, big round shiny kitten eyes. She only talks in the dream and speaks to Pupu with তুমি.
**Rules:** she never knows more than Pupu (she wonders, guesses and encourages, but never explains or foreshadows); she is always in full colour (she is real, not a memory); she always stays a little ahead, waits for Pupu, and only speaks when she has landed on solid ground; she never walks on air.
**Moods:** excited (sparkles, perked ears), curious (head tilt), worried (flattened ears, sweat drop), happy (closed smiling eyes, blush).

## Dadu · দাদু
Pupu's late grandfather, seen in memories. Bald with white tufts at the sides, a full white beard, round reading glasses, a white tupi, a sky-blue panjabi, a green checked lungi, brown sandals and his lathi (walking stick). Warm, patient and funny; his face shows through the beard: bushy white brows, smile crinkles, a laughing grin. His lessons: "আবার চেষ্টা করো" ("Try again"), and "fix things with care and patience, and even broken things sing again".

## Ma · মা
Pupu's mother: a bun, a peacock-blue sari with a turmeric border and a maroon blouse; at night she checks the house with her phone torch. Strict about bedtime, but never cruel.

## Baba · বাবা
Pupu's father: white vest, blue checked lungi, square glasses, a thick moustache. Shows up when the noise is too big to ignore.

## Anko Sir · অঙ্ক স্যার
The maths teacher of Chapter 2, giant in the dream. Bald on top with combed black side hair, a thick moustache, heavy black square glasses whose lenses glow like lamps (orange when he is alert) and sweep the room like searchlights, a white short-sleeved shirt with two pens in the pocket, grey trousers, a black belt with a gold buckle, and a long wooden ruler. He shouts "What? What? WHAT?!" before every chalk throw. When he finds Pupu's homework, he shrinks back into an ordinary teacher who rewards trying. When the magic door appears, he decides he "didn't see anything" and needs some sleep.

## Jhumpa · ঝুম্পা
A classmate who helps Pupu escape. Long, thick, wavy dark hair in a low ponytail, a side-swept fringe, bold black glasses, a white shirt with puffed sleeves and a navy pinafore; brave and quick-thinking. Built on the same skeleton and walk cycle as Pupu. She waits with Anko Sir while Pupu goes through the magic door.

## Professor Kalo-Bijli · প্রফেসর কালো-বিজলি
The villain of *Thunder Boy*, Pupu's favourite cartoon ("Kalo-Bijli" means black lightning); in every episode he tries to steal Thunder Boy's lightning, and Pupu has always wanted to teach him a lesson. Chapter 3 takes place in his laboratory. A long face with a sharp nose and a pointed goatee, a thin curled moustache, a tired dark circle under his eye (it glows yellow when he is angry or laughing), wild black hair with white streaks standing up like lightning with little sparks crackling in it, brass goggles with cyan lenses pushed up on his forehead, and a black high-collared lab coat with purple lining and a yellow lightning badge. He talks from his **live video screen** (with static, a LIVE light and his name). **Moods:** smug (a smirk), laugh (big open grin), angry (brows down, gritted teeth), shock (eyes wide, mouth an O), zapped (eyes crossed out, hair frizzed and smoking, soot on his face). His spark-bugs work for him. Zapped by his own lightning when Pupu takes back Dadu's radio: "I'll get you for this, Pupu… I will!"

## Montu-da · মন্টুদা
The tea seller at the riverside stall where Dadu and Pupu had tea and bread every day. Messy black hair, a thick moustache, a white panjabi under a green vest, a green checked lungi; he stirs the kettle with a long spoon.

## Creatures
- **The naughty crow:** lives in a tree full of crows and steals Pupu's cap.
- **The crab:** guards a pillar on the ghat; jump on its back to knock it over.
- **Spark-bugs (বিজলি-পোকা):** Kalo-Bijli's little helpers: crackling balls of blue light with big eyes and tiny legs, running back and forth along the lab's floor cables. Jump over them; they pop away when the power is rerouted.
