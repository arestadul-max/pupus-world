# Characters · চরিত্র

All characters are drawn in code in the Rongpat style: bold ink outlines, flat colour, a long almond "fish" eye in profile, lively brows and mouths. **Every character shows an expression when they talk** (angry, sad, scared, grinning, suspicious, surprised, happy), and **no character ever slides**: every move uses a real walk or run cycle matched to its speed.

## Pupu · পুপু
The hero, 13–14. Slim, backwards red cap (never in bed), a band-aid on his cheek, red T-shirt, indigo knee shorts; barefoot at home, white canvas sneakers in the dream (barefoot as a little boy in memories). Imaginative, a little mischievous, brave when it counts; he misses Dadu more than he says.
**Moves:** walk, run (knees high, arms pumping), crouch-walk, jump with anticipation and landing, sit (side and front), squat to pick up, throw, swing on roots, hide, sleep curled up.

## Mishti · মিষ্টি
Pupu's ginger tabby cat: orange coat with dark stripes, cream belly, pink nose, a slim red collar with a little gold bell, big round shiny kitten eyes. She only talks in the dream and speaks to Pupu with তুমি.
**Rules:** she never knows more than Pupu (she wonders, guesses and encourages, but never explains or foreshadows); she is always in full colour (she is real, not a memory); she always stays a little ahead, waits for Pupu, and only speaks when she has landed on solid ground; she never walks on air.
**Moods:** excited (sparkles, perked ears), curious (head tilt), worried (flattened ears, sweat drop), happy (closed smiling eyes, blush).

## Dadu · দাদু
Pupu's late grandfather, seen in memories. Bald with white tufts at the sides, a full white beard, round reading glasses, a white tupi, a sky-blue panjabi, a green checked lungi, brown sandals and his lathi (walking stick). Warm, patient and funny; his face shows through the beard: bushy white brows, smile crinkles, a laughing grin. His lesson: "আবার চেষ্টা করো" ("Try again").

## Ma · মা
Pupu's mother: a bun, a peacock-blue sari with a turmeric border and a maroon blouse; at night she checks the house with her phone torch. Strict about bedtime, but never cruel.

## Baba · বাবা
Pupu's father: white vest, blue checked lungi, square glasses, a thick moustache. Shows up when the noise is too big to ignore.

## Anko Sir · অঙ্ক স্যার
The maths teacher of Chapter 2, giant in the dream. Bald on top with combed black side hair, a thick moustache, heavy black square glasses whose lenses glow like lamps (orange when he is alert) and sweep the room like searchlights, a white short-sleeved shirt with two pens in the pocket, grey trousers, a black belt with a gold buckle, and a long wooden ruler. He shouts "What? What? WHAT?!" before every chalk throw. When he finds Pupu's homework, he shrinks back into an ordinary teacher who rewards trying.

## Jhumpa · ঝুম্পা
A classmate who helps Pupu escape. Long, thick, wavy dark hair in a low ponytail, a side-swept fringe, bold black glasses, a white shirt with puffed sleeves and a navy pinafore; brave and quick-thinking. Built on the same skeleton and walk cycle as Pupu.

## Montu-da · মন্টুদা
The tea seller at the riverside stall where Dadu and Pupu had tea and bread every day. Messy black hair, a thick moustache, a white panjabi under a green vest, a green checked lungi; he stirs the kettle with a long spoon.

## Creatures
- **The naughty crow:** lives in a tree full of crows and steals Pupu's cap.
- **The crab:** guards a pillar on the ghat; jump on its back to knock it over.
