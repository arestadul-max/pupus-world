## Style rules (agreed)
- **Subtitles always fit their box (v216):** `drawDialog` wraps the Bangla and English lines by words (`wrapLines`) and the box grows to fit; the Bangla still types out across its lines.
- **Anko Sir can be in only one place at a time (v216):** when he bursts out of the classroom door, the window watch stops (no face at any window during or after the burst).
- **No round medallion / logo / emblem on any door** (v211, user's rule): doors have plain panels, a handle and nothing else.
- **Code rule (v211):** never start a new level (anything that calls `resetAll()` or `play()`) from inside a cutscene `{do: …}` step — runCS clears `G.cs` when the old scene ends and would wipe the new level's opening scene (this froze the Part 1 → Part 2 change). Start it from the scene's `after` callback: `play([...], () => startVer())`.
- **Every sitting object must be sittable** (E/USE: sit on the …). Front-facing seats (benches, sofas) use the front sit pose; side-view chairs use the side sit pose. Lists `SEATS` (prologue) and `GH_SEATS` (Chapter 1), `front:true` for front-facing.
- **Every human has hands like Pupu's** (small outlined oval; helpers `arm3`/`handAt`), holding things believably.
- **Rural Bangladesh, no Hindu motifs** (no temple domes, bells, marigolds, diyas, sky lanterns, alpana). Muslim/rural details are fine (tupi caps, lungi, panjabi).
- Rongpat folk-art look: bold ink outlines (#1C1410), flat colour, single long almond "fish" eye in profile, lively brows/mouths, blinking, talking mouth flap.
- Fonts: Rozha One (display), Hind Siliguri (Bangla/body).
- **HUD must never block the scenery:** compact pills at the top-left (memory-cup pill 172×28 with cup icon + 10 dots; goal pill sized to its text; no chapter title in the HUD); hints are a small banner (13 px) at y≈44; toasts small at y≈84.
- Objects keep small gaps between them; nothing floats unsupported.
- Poster/sticker texts English only. Bangla only where it belongs (dialogue, signs like the tea menu).
- **Mishti uses তুমি** (never তুই), never walks in mid-air, only interrupts with tips when Pupu is on calm ground (`calmGround()`: on the ground, not crumbling, not on a moving thing) and **always stays ahead of Pupu** (waypoints `MWP`; she moves on as soon as he catches up).
- **Control instructions:** the prologue teaches basic controls; afterwards plain walking/running tips are not repeated (the pause menu lists the controls). **But every new mechanic/challenge must say clearly which button to press**, using the `kl()` key labels: [Space]/[E]/[Move] become Space/E/← → on PC and JUMP/USE/◀ ▶ on phones. In-world prompts above Pupu ("E: pick up a stone") are also used.
- Boats: Pupu can't walk off any boat without jumping, and his feet stay inside the hull (`edge` margin). Paper boat layering: back paper wall → paper sail (cone) → Pupu & cat → front paper wall.

## Instructions (RULE, v221 — replaces the v187 pop-up cards)
- **Instructions never pause the game.** Each button the player needs appears as a small pulsing key chip floating above Pupu (key + a one-word action label, e.g. "Shift / sprint", "Space / jump", "↑↓/WS / walk in / out"). A chip disappears the moment the player does that action (crouch, jump, use, sprint, move, depth) or after its time runs out (`showHint` → `G.keyHint`, `keyHintDone`, `updateKeyHints`, `drawKeyHints`).
- A chip's label can be set per hint with `[Shift:hide]`: only where the button is used to hide (behind the big courtyard tree), the chip says **C / hide**; everywhere else it stays **C / crouch** (v222, narrowed in v224).
- **A chip stays until the player does that action** (no timeout), and stays at least 1.5 s so it is never skipped because the player was already doing it (v226: the sprint chip in the chase used to vanish at once because Pupu was already sprinting). Chips are cleared when a cutscene starts and are hidden during cutscenes and conversations. After being caught in the chase, the chase chips and the tree “hide” chip show again.
- Hints with no button (warnings such as "Ma is getting up…") show as one short dark line at the top of the screen for a few seconds (`G.infoHint`), never a card over the scene.
- **Every key in a tip shows the right button for the device** (v205): write keys as `[E]`, `[Space]`, `[Shift]` (= crouch: C / SNEAK), `[Move]`, `[Depth]`, `[Run]` (= sprint: Shift on PC, RUN on phone) — `kl()` turns them into key chips; where the action differs (hold Shift on PC vs tap the RUN toggle on a phone) the sentence itself changes with `isTouch`. Never write raw key names or "(on a phone …)" asides.
- **Instructions appear just BEFORE the player starts that task** (e.g. crab tip while riding the paper boat toward the crab's pillar; crumbling-stones tip while standing on the pillar; crouch tip just before the first bench in Chapter 2; depth tip before the back row).
- In Chapter 2 Pupu never stands up under a bench, also not in cutscenes or while someone talks (drawn crouched whenever `crouchOn`); in the intro he crouches first, then creeps under the bench.

## Talking expressions (RULE for every character, v186)
**Every character shows an expression whenever they talk** (`talkExpr(default)` reads the line being said; a line can also set `mood:'sad'` etc.): angry (shouting, scolding), sad (sorry, miss, aww), scared (ouch, shh, caught), grin (ha ha, won, wow, got it), suspicious (hmm, I know you're here), surprised (questions), happy (exclamations). Used by Pupu (and kid Pupu in memories), Ma, Baba, Dadu, Montu-da and Anko Sir (own brows: shouting slam-down + anger marks / one raised brow / both up; mouth size changes); Mishti has her own moods. **Dadu's face shows through his beard and glasses:** bushy white brow just above the glasses that moves with the mood, smile crinkles, and a mouth under the moustache (warm smile opening as he talks, big laughing grin, frown when sad/angry, round "O" when surprised).

## Mishti behaviour system (MUST follow in every chapter — user: "always save this system so no mistake happens")
- She **always stays ahead** of Pupu: walks/hops to her next waiting spot (`MWP`) and **waits there for him**.
- She **only speaks when she is standing still at her waiting spot** on solid ground/a leaf — never while walking, never mid-hop, never in the air. A tip that triggers while she is still travelling is **queued** (`G.mQueue`) and spoken the moment she has arrived (`mishtiSettled()`) and Pupu is on calm ground (`calmGround()`).
- **Order at every obstacle: arrive → wait for Pupu → give the instruction → then jump/walk on** (e.g. on the last lotus leaf she gives the paper-boat tip first, and only then hops onto the boat).
- If any cutscene starts while she is mid-hop, she **finishes the hop and lands first** (`mishtiAir()`); she is never frozen in the air.
- She never walks in mid-air; she uses real paths, jumps between real surfaces, or rides things. She speaks to Pupu with তুমি.

## Controls (all levels)
- PC: A/D or ← → move. **W/S and ↑/↓ do nothing in flat (2D) levels; only levels with depth (Chapter 2's 2.5D rooms) use them to walk into / out of the room, and only there do the phone ▲ ▼ buttons appear** (pause menu lists W S only in depth levels). Space (or K) jump, Shift (hold) sprint, C toggles crouch, E use/hide/sit/pick up/aim/throw, P pause, M mute, F fullscreen.
- Phone (landscape): ◀ ▶, JUMP, RUN (toggle), SNEAK (toggle), USE (glows when usable), fullscreen button (first tap also asks for fullscreen). During the stone-skipping contest the move/jump buttons hide (tap anywhere to throw).
- Pause: "Paused" how-to-play card with Resume (P) and Main menu (Q / tap).

## Physics / collision (v139)
- Surfaces `GH_SURF` with flags floor / stairs / pad (lotus) / boat / mover / crumble; `surfYc` clamps height past the ends.
- **Ledge forgiveness `LEDGE = 10`**: feet have width, he only drops once more than half a foot past an edge (boats excluded: they clamp).
- **Swept landing**: the crossing point of the surface height during the frame is checked, so fast jumps that clear an edge land correctly.
- Swinging roots: pendulum (`SWING_G 1500`, pump 3.6 with ← →, energy capped at about 60° amplitude), **swept grab test** over the whole frame path using hands (plus a small reach), head, shoulders and chest; can grab any time (also after the cup); re-grab cooldown only for the root just released.
