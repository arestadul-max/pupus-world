## 1.1.0-demo · 2026-11-12
Chapter 3 · The Science Lab.

- **Bridge from Chapter 2:** at the school gate a power cut darkens the yard; a lab window sparks blue with a golden glow inside; Anko Sir hands Pupu the lab key.

## 1.0.0-demo · 2026-11-11
First public demo: the Prologue, Chapter 1 and Chapter 2.

- **Languages:** play fully in English or fully in Bangla (start menu).
- **Teaching:** first-steps tutorial (move, run, crouch) in the Prologue; a goal line above Pupu with the keys to use and an arrow showing the way; glowing objects to interact with; short tips after mistakes; one message at a time, never repeated.
- **Surprise actions:** a big pop-up (R on PC, tap on phones) to hide behind the schoolyard tree and to dodge falling books; all other buttons hide meanwhile.
- **Memories:** Dadu's skipping-stone contest (Chapter 1) and Dadu's maths puzzle game (Chapter 2), new numbers, pattern and riddle every time, in Dadu's lamp-lit room.
- **Look:** Rongpat folk-art style throughout; new stairwell and Dadu's room lighting; Mishti with big shiny kitten eyes.
- **Phone:** joystick (push a little to walk, all the way to run), full jump on any tap, installable as an app.
